# BA EX Buff Timeline

A planner for Blue Archive raid rotations: lay out EX and Basic skill casts on a timeline, see buff and debuff windows,
what each cast does to the carry, and the team's cost over time.

Open `index.html` through any static web server (for example `python -m http.server`) or use the hosted copy.
Your teams are saved in your browser; use Export / Import to move them.

- Student and skill data come from [SchaleDB](https://schaledb.com) (`data.js` is generated by `gen_data.py`; the boss data in `bosses.js` by `gen_boss.py`, from SchaleDB's raid files kept in `research/`). SchaleDB has no boss cast times, so boss skills are placed by hand.
- Each student card in the Team tab has a **Skills** button that opens a window with their skills the way SchaleDB shows them (same wording, coloured numbers, buff icons, range / cooldown pills); it is for reference only and reads each skill at the levels set on the student's card (EX Lv, Basic Lv, Sub Lv). The texts live in `skills.js`, made by `gen_skills.py` from the same `research/students.json` plus SchaleDB's English strings (`research/schaledb-localization.json`); the page fetches it the first time a window is opened. After a data refresh run `python gen_data.py research/students.json` and `python gen_skills.py research/students.json`, then bump the `?v=` numbers in `index.html`.
- **Skill activations** (Team tab): for a Basic Skill the rules cannot place (Fubuki's Every Last Round! fires once her ammo is 3 or less) you add each use by hand, pick the student and press + Add activation, then drag the bar in her Basic Skill row (or type a time in the list). A Basic Skill that stuns, frightens or provokes (Fubuki, Karin, Kayoko, Konoka, Moe, Hina and Chise in swimsuit) draws a bar for how long that lasts, so you can see when to use something that needs the enemy stunned; the lengths per Basic Lv are SchaleDB's (`cc` in `data.js`). They are saved with the team (`acts`). While you drag one of these bars (or a boss event) two dashed lines mark its start and end across the whole timeline, each with a tag for the clock time and the team's cost at that moment.
- **Neru (School)'s mark**: her normal EX ("You Want to Fight?") puts her in a Duel state with an enemy for 70 s. The timeline draws it as a "Marked 70s" bar on her lane (dashed, because it is an estimate) and the details of her casts give the start and end times. While she is marked the EX card she draws can only be the follow-up "This'll Hurt, Y'know" (cost 4), so every cast inside the mark is set to it automatically, and the first one after the mark is her normal EX again; a form you pick in the table always wins. SchaleDB's data has no timing for the mark. What the two Wakamo recordings show: her new card appears when the cast animation ends (4.4 s after the cost is spent), and her card went back to "You Want to Fight?" 71.6 s after the cast in one (Rio's copy of it, 70.5 s in the other), so the tool starts the 70 s 1.1 s (33 frames) into the cast. Two recordings 1.1 s apart, so treat the times as an estimate.
- **Cards in hand** (Rotation tab): once the five starting skills are set in the Team tab, a row under every cast shows the three EX cards in your hand after it (the three in your hand are bright, the cards waiting behind them grey, the card just drawn is outlined; Rio's copy has a glowing frame), with a line of text when the cast does something special (Rio's copy, a card redrawn at once, a card that is not in the hand). The rule, checked against a Hod Yellow Torment recording (every one of 25 draws matched): a pressed card's slot is refilled from the front of a queue and the pressed card goes to the back; the queue starts as the 4th and 5th starting skill, then the student not in the first five. Rio's EX turns her card, in place, into a glowing copy of the target's card; to use that copy pick "<student> (Copy)" in the cast's Student box (the rows after Rio's cast offer the one copy she holds, until it is used; Rio can only copy a Striker, so her Target box lists only Strikers; a plain student is always the real card; a cast with "copy" in its Note still counts as a copy until the Student box is set). A copy stays the copied student's cast (her lane, her animation: while she is busy the copy cannot go off either), costs the original's base cost minus 1 (then reductions apply), and follows the card it copies (Neru's copy is the follow-up while she is marked and changes back to her normal EX when the mark ends); it does not start a mark of its own. Skills that "immediately redraw" the EX card put it straight back into the same slot instead of sending it to the back; this is checked in recordings for Neru (School) (her first EX, which comes back as the follow-up), Aris (Armed) (Sword of Light, not Supernova) and Hina (Dress) (the stance cast and the first two notes, not The Final Song). For Toki, Hanako (Swimsuit), Hiyori (Swimsuit), Mika (Swimsuit), Shun (Swimsuit) and Ibuki (Swimsuit) the redraw depends on a gauge or stance the tool does not follow and has not been recorded: their casts get a red line under the cards saying the cards after them may be wrong. A red outline and red line mean the card is not in the hand at that point. Untick "Show cards in hand under each cast" to hide the rows and the opening hand above the table while you play a rotation (the choice is kept in your browser).

## Tests

`node tests/run_all.js <students.min.json>` (get the file from https://schaledb.com/data/en/students.min.json). Needs Node 18+, nothing to install.

- `tests/audit_students.js`: every student in `data.js` against SchaleDB (coverage, costs, buff durations, icons, duplicate names) and a list of every cost mechanic in the game that the tool does or does not model.
- `tests/skills.js`: the Skills window reads exactly as on SchaleDB (Mika's five skills word for word), with the right icons, tags and pills, and every skill of every student draws at every level with no leftover markup.
- `tests/audit_bosses.js`: every Total Assault boss, difficulty and terrain (HP, time limit, armor, skills, portraits), how Grand Assault seasons use the bosses, and boss effects that touch cost.
- `tests/invariants.js`: rules that must hold for any team (cost never above the cap, planned casts affordable, settling twice changes nothing, a reload does not move casts, nothing slow), run on random teams, every student and every boss.

The engine is loaded straight out of `index.html`, so the tests run the code that ships.
